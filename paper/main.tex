\begin{abstract}
We prove a native finite theory of relational saturation in the
registered Thalean mechanics built over
\[
\Gfifteen\cong L(\Petersen).
\]
The result separates three operations that were previously treated
only conjecturally as parts of one saturation architecture:
native selection, contextual retention, and quadratic contrast
concentration.

For the certified registered carrier language, admissible prefix
extension contracts the lawful completion family exactly as
\[
8\longrightarrow2\longrightarrow1.
\]
We define native relational loading as this monotone accumulation of
admissible constraint.  Registered-future saturation occurs when the
completion family becomes a singleton.  The unique registered future
does not collapse the complete relational state: the two-edge stage
still contains \(120\) distinct contexts, and the terminal completion
register retains two native holonomy designations above each of the
\(15\) closing chambers.

The native designation is absent from a bare unresolved chamber but
becomes operational after one admitted carrier step.  If
\(\Delta\) denotes the resulting context-activated \(b/ab\)
designation response and \(H\) the exact ten-dimensional
five-address-hidden projector, then
\[
\Delta^{\mathsf T}\Delta
=
\Delta\Delta^{\mathsf T}
=
\frac34 H,
\qquad
\rho\Delta=0.
\]
Thus native loading selects exactly a retained relational sector that
is invisible to the declared five-address readout.

This native process is not the same as common-mode contrast
concentration.  Under the canonical uniform completion-occupancy
register, every one of the \(180\) strict loading extensions increases
the Paper 14 contrast fraction,
\[
\frac{31}{55}
\longrightarrow
\frac{41}{65}
\longrightarrow
\frac{7}{10}.
\]
Native loading therefore sharpens the selected future.

Separately, the Thalean quadratic
\[
Q=MM^{\mathsf T}=A^3+2A^2+2I
\]
has common-mode eigenvalue \(98\), while every centered eigenvalue is
at most \(18\).  Its canonical normalization
\[
\widehat Q=\frac1{98}Q
\]
fixes the common mode and contracts centered contrast by the sharp
factor \(9/49\) per iterate.  Every finite iterate remains invertible.
The native loading response and the centered Thalean quadratic lie in
the same exact three-dimensional transport-response algebra, but they
perform distinct operations.

The resulting finite theorem is therefore not
``loading equals quadratic averaging.''  It is a sharper architecture:
admissible selection reduces future freedom, contextual transport
retains distinctions hidden by registration, and the quadratic
supplies an independent contrast-concentrating operation without
finite-stage erasure.

The result is entirely finite and internal.  No identification is
made with physical gravity, black holes, spacetime horizons,
singularity avoidance, Planck-scale information density, or the
cosmic microwave background.
\end{abstract}
